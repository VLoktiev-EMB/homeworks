<objective>
Extend the reference project with the `esp32s3_arduino` environment and prove two facts at the
artifact level: N16R8 (16 MB flash, octal PSRAM) and the participation of the shared libraries in
the CDB. This is the first of the two ESP scenarios; it runs before ESP-IDF because the Arduino
builder is simpler and needs no `sdkconfig` (D-19).

Purpose: criteria 1, 2 and 4 cannot be proven by reading an ini file. The evidence is
`esptool image-info`, `qio_opi/include` and `ARDUINO_PARTITION_default_16MB` in the CDB, plus
`BOARD_HAS_PSRAM`, which without D-22 is absent from every translation unit (P5).

Output: the `[env:esp32s3_arduino]` section in `homeworks/0_0-toolchain-smoke/platformio.ini`, a
green Arduino build, CDB evidence, and 16 MB image evidence.
</objective>
