<objective>
Add the second ESP environment, `esp32s3_espidf`, with the two mandatory project artifacts without
which the ESP-IDF build does not start: `default_16MB.csv` and `sdkconfig.defaults`. Prove 16 MB
flash and octal PSRAM through `sdkconfig.json` and the image header, and prove the participation of
the shared libraries through the CDB.

by the `board_build.*` keys alone, because IDF is configured through Kconfig (P4). A `-D` in
`build_flags` never reaches Kconfig, so `sdkconfig.defaults` is the only working route. The research
reproduced both failure modes exactly: removing the project-local CSV aborts the build on a missing
partition table file, and removing `sdkconfig.defaults` leaves the build green while the image is
configured for 2 MB — which is why SUCCESS is never accepted as proof here.

Output: `[env:esp32s3_espidf]`, `default_16MB.csv`, `sdkconfig.defaults`, a green ESP-IDF build, and
artifact-level evidence.
