## Decisions Made

- Keep the script instead of documenting a bare command: the script prevents a hand-typed
  commit and a hard-coded `origin/main` (research A4 recommendation).
- `set -euo pipefail` differs from the `set -uo pipefail` of `review_smoke_test.sh` and
  `new_homework` because this script has no aggregate status to collect; errexit is the
  fail-closed form here.
- The merged-PR gate and the directory-at-commit gate become review rules; git's own
  refusals keep the "created once, never moved" property. `AGENTS.md` must be rewritten to
  match (plan 04.1-04).
