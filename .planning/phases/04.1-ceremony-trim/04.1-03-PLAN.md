test that would otherwise fail the new decision table.

keeps that grant in history. Do not claim the repository "was always MIT".

real identity; it is deleted with the files it guarded. `LICENSE` itself keeps the handle and
the identity rule survives in `AGENTS.md` as review-enforced.

Purpose: remove the licence-compliance pipeline and the ADR index assertion before the
document rewrites.
Output: one MIT licence; one gitleaks CI job; the three coupled tests absent.
</objective>
