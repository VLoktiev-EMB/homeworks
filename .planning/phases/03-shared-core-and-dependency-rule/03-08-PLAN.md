<objective>
Turn the dependency rule into a failing check: a textual `#include` scanner with a hardened
classifier, a deny-by-default allowlist, the `platformio.ini` closure rules, a public-header contract
test, and a local pre-commit hook that also covers the checker's own data.

Purpose: ARCH-05 requires enforcement, and research proved that the compiler cannot supply it — a
relative include from `common` into an adapter compiles and links natively, and an include such as
`common/../arduino/...` resolves into the adapter while its first segment still says `common`. The
textual checker is therefore load-bearing: it enforces source intent, while the native build enforces
transitive purity. Both are needed, and this plan delivers the first with a classifier that cannot be
walked around by path arithmetic.

Output: `scripts/check_architecture.py`, `scripts/architecture/allowlist.json`, two Python test
modules covering the checker and the header surface, and one new `repo: local` pre-commit hook.
</objective>
