<objective>
Implement the three contracts that have a real consumer on both frameworks — time, GPIO and
logging — as thin `.c` translations, and prove each compiles against the pinned toolchain.

Purpose: ARCH-03 wants thin adapters and deterministic doubles, not a framework wrapper. This phase
has no homework and therefore no consumer for I2C or SPI on hardware; writing those adapters now
would be a speculative implementation shaped around one guess. D-08 defers them, and the contracts
already exist for the first homework that needs one.

Output: six header/source pairs under `lib/arduino/` and `lib/espidf/`, each proven by a target
compile and a compilation-database entry.
</objective>
