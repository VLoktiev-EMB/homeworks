<objective>
Complete the `lib/port` contract surface with GPIO, I2C, SPI and logging, and prove each one —
including the time contract from plan 03-01 — is callable through its vtable with a caller-owned
context.

Purpose: ARCH-01 names five contracts, and ARCH-07 forbids the shape that would make them a shadow
framework. Writing all five now, while no consumer exists, risks shaping them around a guess — so the
plan deliberately keeps I2C and SPI at the bus level (address, buffer, length) and defers every
driver concept to the homework that needs one. Plan 03-06 implements only time, GPIO and logging on
the real frameworks, which is the honest consumer set for this phase (D-08).

Output: four new headers under `lib/port/include/port/` and one Unity suite that exercises all five
contracts.
</objective>
