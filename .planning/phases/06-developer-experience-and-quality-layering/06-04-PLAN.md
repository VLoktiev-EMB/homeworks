## Flagged Assumptions (Specless Probe Fallback)

- **D-08a scope gap (flagged, not resolved):** native test translation units are not described by any compilation database today. `pio test` exposes no compiledb target, and a test TU borrows an include path that lacks `unity_config.h`. The pre-push scope is therefore `lib/common`, `lib/port`, and `lib/host`; the gap is recorded in ADR 0013 and handed to Phase 8, where CI owns database generation. Do not hand-maintain include flags to close it.
- **Port-header translation-unit gap (flagged, resolved by probe):** `tests/compile_commands.json` contains no translation unit for `lib/port`, so a standalone port header may have no compile command. The Task 1 header probe decides between the widened pattern (header analysable through a borrowed command) and the documented narrowing branch; either way the guard keeps the wide pattern and ADR 0013 records the outcome.
- **TOOL-01 idempotency (flagged, resolved by gate):** re-running the guard and the clang-tidy hook over an unchanged tree reports the same verdict and writes no file. The probe's restore step plus `git status --porcelain lib/common/src/hex.c` being empty is the mechanical evidence.
