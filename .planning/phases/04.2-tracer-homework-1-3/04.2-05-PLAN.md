**Evidence is hybrid (D-11):** the real board for build, flash, and serial, plus three real
measurements — blink period, LED current, and supply voltage — and `Vf` in the multimeter's
diode mode. The user operates the instruments; the executor records what the user reports. If
an instrument is unavailable, the row is `not measured` with the reason. `not measured` is an
acceptable and expected value, not a failure.

**The five labels are fixed by D-13.** `measured`, `calculated`, `simulated`, `datasheet`, and
`not measured`. `observed` is not a sixth label; D-14's debounce result is
`measured (observed)` and the distinction is explained in the methodology.

because `WOKWI_CLI_TOKEN` is absent. Any expected timing derived from the scenario is
`simulated (expected, not observed)`. Do not upgrade it.
