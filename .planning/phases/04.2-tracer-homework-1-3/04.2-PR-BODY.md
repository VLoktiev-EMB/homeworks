## What and why

This pull request delivers homework 1.3 (Police Flasher) end to end as the phase 4.2
tracer, and with it the phase's primary output: a friction log that Phases 5 through 11
must justify their automation against. The homework is scaffolded through
`scripts/new_homework`, builds for the ESP32-S3 Arduino environment, carries a
homework-local `lib/pattern` library with 38 native Unity cases, a canonical
`assets/diagram.json` and Wokwi scenario, a ten-section report with BOM, pin map,
calculations, measurement ledger, traceability matrix, and decision appendix, and one
single-project CI workflow. The two manual integration edits are the root `README.md`
index row and the `homeworks.code-workspace` folder; `platformio.shared.ini` deliberately
gains no per-homework entry.

The friction log — `.planning/phases/04.2-tracer-homework-1-3/04.2-FRICTION-LOG.md` — records
18 seven-field entries covering scaffold, code, simulation, report, integration, and CI,
maps 13 automation candidates to an owning phase or a recorded decision not to build, and
dispositions 14 edge probes. This is a first pass: the tag, the polished pilot, the
simulation ADR, the full CI matrix, and the report checker remain with Phases 7, 8, 10, and
11.
