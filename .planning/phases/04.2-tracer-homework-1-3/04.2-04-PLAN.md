**Wiring facts to state, not invent:** the single-GPIO half-bridge is 150 ohm from GPIO4 to
node X, LED1 anode X and cathode GND, LED2 anode 3V3 and cathode X; the third LED is GPIO6
through 150 ohm to GND; the button is GPIO7 to GND with the internal pull-up. GPIO5 is reserved
for the alternative three-LED profile. The diagram declares `flashSize: "16"`,
`psramType: "octal"`, and `psramSize: "8"`.

**Half-bridge fidelity:** the all-off frame drives GPIO4 to a high-impedance input, not to a
driven level. On hardware the shared node floats near the LED clamp voltage, so "both LEDs
dark" is a simulation approximation, and the off LED's reverse voltage is not well-defined in
that state. State this in the theory section and in the reverse-voltage calculation's
assumption-effect column; plan 05 records it in the decision appendix and the ledger.

**Calculation inputs:** the supply is nominally 3.3 V from the board regulator (datasheet or
measured in plan 05), the LED forward voltage is a datasheet value until plan 05 measures it,
and the target current is a design choice near 10 mA. The ESP32-S3 per-pin absolute maximum is
40 mA. State the assumption that both LEDs of the pair are the same red type, because unequal
forward voltages make the two halves unequal in brightness.

**Commit hash:** run `git rev-parse --short HEAD` before writing and record that hash in the
metadata table as the implementation commit the report describes. Do not write a placeholder.
