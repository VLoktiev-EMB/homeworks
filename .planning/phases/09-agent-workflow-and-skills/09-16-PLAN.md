<objective>
Close the contract half of G-09-1: the flash skill must resolve one serial port, pass it to both
the upload and the capture, and explain why the two ESP32-S3 serial paths differ.

The helper change in `09-15` makes a capture deterministic only when the caller also targets the
same interface it flashed. The board exposes two paths: the native USB-Serial-JTAG port
(`USB VID:PID=303A:1001`), where baud is ignored and DTR/RTS are reset controls, and the CH343
UART bridge (`USB VID:PID=1A86:55D3`), where baud is significant and DTR/RTS drive the auto-reset
circuit. With both cables attached, two candidates exist and PlatformIO's implicit selection can
flash one while the monitor opens the other. The skill currently never resolves or records the
port, so the user cannot tell which interface produced a log.

Purpose: make the announced port the executed port, and make an ambiguous candidate set a stop
rather than a silent choice.
Output: the port-resolution contract, the explicit upload and capture commands, the two-path
documentation, the shared guardrails rule, dated records, and the regenerated mirror.

Design choice: resolve the port in the skill before the gate, pass it explicitly to both
commands, and keep the helper's own fail-closed resolution as the backstop for direct
invocations. The canonical tree stays the only authored source; the mirror is regenerated.
</objective>
