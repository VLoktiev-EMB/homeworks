<objective>
Close GAP 1 / CR-03 and fold the in-scope part of IN-06, without reopening any earlier work.

The shipped helper already accepts `--baud` and `_build_command` already appends `-b <n>` to
the monitor. The defect is entirely on the caller side: `.claude/skills/hw-flash/SKILL.md`
announces a capture command with no baud option and tells the agent to pass `-b <n>`, which is
the monitor flag, not a helper flag. In the absent-`monitor_speed` branch the run therefore
either drops the baud (PlatformIO's forbidden 9600) or exits 2, while the gate announces a
different baud than the one executed.

This is the run's tracer because the whole chain — skill prose, helper flag, monitor argv — is
runnable without hardware and is exactly where the blocker lives.

Purpose: make the announced baud the executed baud on both the declared and the absent-setting
paths, and leave a test that fails if the forwarding regresses again.
Output: corrected `hw-flash` prose and commands, `_build_command` and CLI-payload tests, a
concurrent-reader test, the regenerated mirror and manifest, and recorded dispositions.
</objective>
